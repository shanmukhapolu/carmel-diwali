Volunteer at Carmel Diwali

The Carmel Diwali Festival of Lights brings the community together through Indian culture, music, dance, food, art, and celebration. Volunteers help make the event possible by welcoming guests, supporting activities, assisting vendors, distributing items, and helping with setup and cleanup.

What volunteers can help with

  • Serve the community; help create a welcoming experience for festival guests.
  • Earn verified service hours; volunteer hours can be documented through the event.
  • Be part of Carmel Diwali; work alongside other volunteers and event organizers.
  • Experience the festival; contribute to the event while being part of the celebration.

Volunteer information: Please select a position and shift that you can reliably attend. Volunteers are expected to arrive on time and remain for their scheduled shift unless an event organizer gives different instructions.
